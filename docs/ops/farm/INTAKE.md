> What we collect from Limor and Tiran, in what form, and the privacy rule. Counts, not names.
> Deck: https://claude.ai/artifact/75R7QoufX1CgxDCqsE7MfY · Plan: https://claude.ai/code/artifact/cbe90acc-79a6-4d39-bc6c-f37ca72933bf

## Privacy rule (non-negotiable)
Participant records contain names of soldiers, Nova survivors and children — health-adjacent, sensitive data.
- We never receive names. Limor/Tiran produce **counts** (or a de-identified sheet: date · program · population · count · partner).
- If raw records must be processed, it happens **on their machine**, and only the aggregate leaves.
- Nothing personal goes into this repo, the doc, the deck, or any application. Ever.

## Meeting plan (45 minutes)
1. Deck, 15 min — what exists in LA for this profile, the targets, the four engines.
2. Five decisions, 15 min — Form 990 · records handover · LADBS status · independent director · host and date for the evening. Each gets an owner and a date.
3. Intake, 15 min — walk this list; mark what exists; agree who sends what by when.

Output of the meeting: this file with ☑ marks, five decisions with dates, and the calendar for weeks 1–4.
