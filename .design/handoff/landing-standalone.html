What it actually does

Not a chat widget. A working ordering line that behaves like your best phone person on their best day.

It takes the whole order

Menu questions, toppings by the half or quarter, the address, the delivery zone and fee, sales tax and the tip. It quotes what it is allowed to quote and the server owns every number that gets charged.

It catches the calls you miss

A call that rings out during the dinner rush is an order that went to somebody else. Jasell texts the caller back automatically, in their language, and picks the order up from there.

Your kitchen sees it immediately

A live kitchen screen, an orders board, and a dashboard for the owner. Orders can require your approval before they start, or run automatically once you trust it.

It speaks to your customers, not at them

Each customer is served in their own language, and their choice sticks for next time. Menus, prices and receipts follow the same rule.

Live in days, not months

You do not change your phone service, print new menus or retrain anyone.

We text-enable your number

You sign one authorization form. Your voice service, your carrier and your number all stay exactly as they are — texting is simply added on top.

We build your menu with you

Categories, dishes, toppings, delivery zones and fees, hours, and the tax rate for your address.

You watch the first orders

Start with every order needing your approval. Turn that off when it has earned it.

Stop paying a commission on people who already know you
